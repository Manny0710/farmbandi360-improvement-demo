FarmBandi360 In-Depth Demo — how to present
============================================

Open: index.html

Flow for the owner meeting
--------------------------
1) SELECT OPTIONS
   - Tick the upgrades you want to pitch
   - Or use presets: Quick wins / Trust / Full UX / Everything
   - Selected chips appear on the right

2) PLAY DEMO
   - Click “Play demo with selected options”
   - Narrator walks step-by-step; phone UI updates to match
   - Use Next / Back, Auto-play, or tap the phone freely
   - Only selected features appear (others stay hidden or show “current site”)

3) SUMMARY
   - Leave-behind list of what you proposed
